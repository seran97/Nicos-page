Buying Guide

3 Steps to Choosing the Right BBQ Grill Cover

  1. Measure Your Grill Precisely Before Ordering

    Built-in grill covers are not one-size-fits-all. Measure the width, depth, and height of your grill island or built-in unit — including any side burners or overhangs — before selecting a size. A cover that's too loose will catch wind and blow off; too tight and it won't seat properly over the grill frame. Most manufacturers, including Covers & All, list exact dimensions on their Amazon product pages, so match them carefully.

  2. Prioritize Material Weight and Coating Type

    For year-round outdoor use, aim for at least 12 oz fabric — but 18 oz PVC-coated material like this pick is the sweet spot for genuine waterproofing and UV resistance without going to the extreme expense of canvas. Avoid covers that only say "water-resistant" if you live in a rainy climate; true waterproofing requires a sealed or coated weave. Check the product specs on Amazon for the actual oz rating, not just marketing language.

  3. Check Review Patterns, Not Just the Star Average

    A 4.6★ average across 213 reviews is meaningful, but dig into the 1- and 2-star reviews to see if there's a pattern — sizing issues, seam failures after one season, or zipper problems are common complaints in this category. For this pick, the negative reviews are mostly sizing mismatches (user error) rather than material failures, which is a good sign. Always read the most recent reviews on Amazon to catch any quality-control changes.
