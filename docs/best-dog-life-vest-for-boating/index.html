Buying Guide

  1. Weigh and measure your dog. Use both weight and chest girth together to pick the right size from the chart.
  2. Practice on dry land first. Let your dog wear the vest around the house before their first swim so it feels normal, not scary.
  3. Check the handle before every trip. Make sure straps are snug and the top handle is easy to grab quickly in an emergency.

FAQs

Can I use this for a dog who's never been on a boat before?

Yes, but let your dog wear it and get comfortable in shallow water first so the boat trip isn't their first experience with both the vest and open water at once.

Does the vest restrict swimming movement?

The cut is designed to allow full leg movement for paddling, so most dogs swim naturally once they get used to the extra buoyancy.

How do I know if it fits correctly?

You should be able to fit two fingers under the belly strap comfortably, and the handle should sit centered over the shoulders so lifting doesn't tip your dog forward.

Is it durable enough for saltwater use?

Yes, the ripstop material handles saltwater fine as long as you rinse it with fresh water after each outing to prevent buildup in the buckles.

Can senior dogs wear this safely?

Yes, and many owners specifically use it for older dogs with less swimming stamina, since the added buoyancy reduces how hard they have to work to stay afloat.
