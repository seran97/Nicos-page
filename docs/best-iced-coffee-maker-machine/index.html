Frequently Asked Questions

How much faster is this than a normal cold brew steep?

The dual filter design lets grounds sit fully saturated, so most batches steep in 8-12 hours versus the 18-24 hours typical of a mason-jar method.

Is the pitcher dishwasher safe?

The glass carafe is dishwasher safe on the top rack; the filter basket is easiest to rinse by hand to keep the mesh clear.

How much coffee does the 1.4L pitcher make?

1.4 liters is roughly 4-5 servings of iced coffee concentrate, which you dilute with water or milk to taste.

Can I use it for hot coffee too?

It's built specifically for cold brew steeping, so it won't work as a hot drip machine, but the concentrate can be poured over ice or warmed separately.

Does it fit in a standard fridge door?

The glass pitcher fits most fridge door shelves and standard shelving without issue, though it's worth measuring tight door compartments first.
