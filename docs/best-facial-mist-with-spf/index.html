Buying Guide: What To Look For

  1. Check the SPF rating fits your exposure level

    SPF 30-50 is fine for daily reapplication, but for prolonged direct sun you'll still want a heavier base sunscreen first.

  2. Look for 'makeup-safe' or 'setting mist' language

    Not every SPF mist is formulated to go over makeup — some will streak foundation, so check reviews specifically for this use case.

  3. Consider scent and finish

    Unscented options pair better with fragrance and other skincare, and a dry-touch finish avoids a shiny midday look.
