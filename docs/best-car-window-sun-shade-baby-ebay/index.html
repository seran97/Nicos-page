Buying Guide: What to Look For

  1. Measure both back windows

    Baby seats often sit off-center, so confirm the shade size covers the actual window your car seat is installed on.

  2. Prioritize cling over clip-on

    Clip-on shades that attach to headrests tend to sag over time; static cling stays flatter against the glass.

  3. Clean the glass before applying

    A quick wipe-down before sticking the cling shade helps it adhere better and last longer.
