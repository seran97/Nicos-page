Buying Guide: 3 Steps to the Right Air Fryer for Corn

Not sure what to look for? Follow these three steps before you buy any air fryer specifically for cooking corn on the cob.

  1. Check the Basket Size First

    A standard ear of corn is 7–9 inches long. Single-basket air fryers under 5 Qt. often can't fit a full ear without snapping it — which leads to uneven cooking. Look for at least a 6 Qt. capacity, or better yet, a dual-basket design like the Chefman's 9 Qt. setup so you can cook multiple ears flat without crowding.

  2. Prioritize Max Temperature

    Corn on the cob benefits from high heat — you want that slight char and caramelization on the kernels, not just steaming. Aim for an air fryer that reaches at least 400°F, ideally 450°F. Lower-temp models will cook the corn but won't give you that roasted flavor that makes air fryer corn worth making in the first place.

  3. Think About Cleanup Before You Buy

    Buttered, seasoned corn leaves residue. Non-stick, dishwasher-safe baskets are a must if you're making corn regularly. Check that the basket coating is PFOA-free and that the manufacturer explicitly confirms dishwasher safety — not all do. The Chefman checks both boxes, which is one reason it scores well with frequent users on Amazon.
