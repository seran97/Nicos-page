Frequently asked questions

Does this work with a Mac?

Yes, the MX Mechanical pairs with macOS via Bluetooth and Logi Options+ software works cross-platform.

Is the backlighting adjustable?

Yes, brightness cycles through several levels and there's an auto-dim feature based on ambient light.

How long does the battery last?

With backlighting on expect roughly 10 days; with it off you can stretch to a few months.

Is this a used or refurbished unit?

Listings vary — always check the specific condition tag on the eBay listing before purchase.

Does it include a USB receiver?

Most units support both Bluetooth and Logi Bolt USB receiver pairing, check the listing photos to confirm what's included.
