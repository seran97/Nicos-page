Frequently Asked Questions

Can this be used anywhere without an outlet nearby?

Yes, that's the entire point — the solar panel makes it fully independent of any outdoor outlet, ideal for fences, sheds, or trees far from power.

How is a rope light different from a bulb string light?

Rope lights encase the LEDs inside a flexible waterproof tube, giving a continuous glow line rather than individual bulb points — good for wrapping around railings or trees.

What are the 8 lighting modes?

They typically include steady-on, slow fade, flash, twinkle, and combination patterns, selectable via a button on the solar panel unit.

How much sun does the panel need daily?

6-8 hours of direct sun gives a full charge for a full night of use; less sun means shorter runtime that night.

Is the rope light flexible enough to wrap tight curves?

Yes, it bends easily around railings, tree trunks, and fence posts without kinking, though very tight loops should be avoided to protect the internal wiring.
