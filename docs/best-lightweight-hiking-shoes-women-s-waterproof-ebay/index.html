Lightweight Hiking Shoes Women'S Waterproof Buying Guide: 3 Steps

Before you buy, run through these three questions so you don't end up with the wrong one.

Confirm Your Usual Shoe Size Against the Listing

Barefoot-style shoes can fit differently than standard sneakers. Check reviews for sizing feedback and consider sizing based on foot length in centimeters if provided.

Break Them In on Short Walks First

Even lightweight, flexible shoes benefit from a few short walks before a long hike, letting your feet and the material adjust to each other.

Reapply Waterproofing Spray Periodically

Waterproof coatings wear down with repeated use and washing. A light reproofing spray every few months of regular hiking keeps performance consistent.
