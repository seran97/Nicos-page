Buying Guide: 3 Steps to Choose the Right Outdoor String Lights

Measure Your Space & Determine Length

Before purchasing outdoor string lights, measure the area you want to illuminate. For patios, pergolas, or backyard spaces up to 25 feet, a single strand like our top pick is perfect. For larger areas, look for lights that can be daisy-chained or purchase multiple strands. Always add a few extra feet to account for draping and hanging slack.

Choose LED Over Incandescent for Longevity

LED Edison bulbs last significantly longer than traditional incandescent options — often 15,000+ hours versus 1,000 hours. They also consume up to 80% less energy, which translates to real savings on your electricity bill. Shatterproof LED bulbs, like those in our top pick, add an extra layer of durability, especially important in outdoor environments where wind and accidental contact are common.

Verify Weather Resistance & Safety Ratings

For outdoor use, always confirm the string lights are rated for outdoor conditions. Look for weather-resistant or waterproof ratings. Check that the cord and sockets are designed to handle moisture, temperature fluctuations, and UV exposure.
