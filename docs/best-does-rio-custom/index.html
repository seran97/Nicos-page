Frequently Asked Questions

Which cameras actually use CFexpress Type A cards?

Type A cards are used mainly in Sony's mirrorless lineup and some Fujifilm bodies; check your camera's manual since Type A and Type B slots are not interchangeable.

Is 240GB enough for 4K video work?

For most projects yes, though shooting long-form 4K raw or high frame rate footage will fill it faster than you'd expect, so budget for regular offloading.

Does the read/write speed actually matter for photographers?

Yes, faster write speeds reduce buffer clearing time during burst shooting, which matters most for sports, wildlife, or fast-action photography.

Will this card overheat during long 4K recording sessions?

Professional-grade cards like this one are built with better heat dissipation than budget cards, but extended 4K sessions in hot environments can still trigger a camera's thermal throttling regardless of card quality.

Is it worth paying more for a professional-grade card over a basic one?

If you shoot professionally or rely on buffer speed during critical moments, yes — the reliability and consistent transfer speeds are worth the premium over budget cards that can bottleneck or corrupt files.
