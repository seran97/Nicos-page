Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between this and other Gourmia models?

This 6-quart digital version sits in the middle of Gourmia's lineup, offering more capacity than their compact 2-4 quart models without stepping up to the bulkier oven-style units.

Can it handle a whole chicken?

A spatchcocked or smaller whole chicken fits, but a large roasting-size bird will need to be cut into pieces first.

Is it energy efficient compared to a full oven?

Owners report noticeably shorter preheat and cook times than a conventional oven, which several mention has visibly lowered their kitchen's summer heat output and energy use.

Does it come with a recipe book?

Most units ship with a basic starter guide, though many owners end up relying on online communities and forums for more detailed recipe ideas.

How do I keep the basket from sticking?

A light spray of oil before adding food, even with a nonstick basket, extends the coating's life and prevents sticking on starchy foods like fries or breaded items.
