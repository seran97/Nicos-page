Buying Guide: How to Pick a Smart Sprinkler Controller

  1. Count your zones. Single hose bib timers work for small yards; multi-zone systems need a dedicated controller.
  2. Check the WiFi range spec against your yard size and how far the unit will be from your router.
  3. Look for manual backup controls in case your WiFi ever goes down mid-season.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the WiFi range really reach 984 feet?

That's the manufacturer's rated range in open-air conditions. Walls and obstructions will reduce it, but most residential yards fall well within usable range.

Can I set different schedules for different zones?

Yes, most smart sprinkler timers let you set independent watering schedules and durations per zone through the companion app.

Does it work without WiFi as a backup?

Most models include manual controls directly on the unit so you can still water your lawn even if your WiFi goes down.

Will it survive being left outside all season?

These timers are built with weather-resistant housings for outdoor faucet use, though it's still good practice to disconnect before hard freezes.

Can I control it remotely when I'm away on vacation?

Yes, that's the main advantage over a manual timer, you can adjust or pause your watering schedule from anywhere using the app.
