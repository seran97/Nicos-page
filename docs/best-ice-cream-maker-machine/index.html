Buying Guide

  1. Freeze the bowl first. Plan ahead — the freezer bowl needs 12 to 24 hours in a standard freezer before use.
  2. Prep your mix ahead of time. Chilled base mixtures churn faster and end up creamier than room-temperature ones.
  3. Add mix-ins near the end. Chocolate chips, fruit, or nuts go in during the last few minutes so they don't sink.

FAQ

How long does it take to make ice cream at home with this machine?

Most batches are ready in about 20-25 minutes once the freezer bowl is properly frozen, though the bowl itself needs 12-24 hours in the freezer beforehand.

Do I need to buy ice or rock salt?

No. This is a freezer-bowl style machine, so there's no ice or salt involved, just a pre-frozen bowl and the mixing paddle.

Can it make sorbet and frozen yogurt too?

Yes, the same bowl and paddle work for sorbet, frozen yogurt, and gelato-style mixes, not just traditional ice cream.

How many people does one batch serve?

The 2-quart capacity typically yields enough for 4-6 servings, depending on how generous the scoops are.

Is it hard to clean afterward?

The bowl, lid, and paddle are designed for easy hand washing, and many parts are dishwasher-safe on the top rack.
