Consider your local humidity. These units perform best in drier climates; humid regions will see less dramatic cooling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size room does this air conditioner cool best?

With a 1600ML water tank and multiple wind speeds, it's best suited to bedrooms, home offices, and dorm rooms rather than open-concept living spaces.

Does it need to be vented out a window?

No, this is an evaporative-style unit that doesn't require window venting, unlike compressor-based portable ACs.

How often do I refill the water tank?

With the 1600ML tank, most users refill roughly once every 6-10 hours of continuous use, depending on room humidity and fan speed.

Can I set it to run only part of the night?

Yes, it includes multiple timer settings so you can have it shut off automatically after a set number of hours.

Is it noisy enough to disrupt sleep?

On lower wind speeds most reviewers describe it as a soft white-noise hum; the highest speed is noticeably louder and better suited to daytime use.
