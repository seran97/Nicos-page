Buying Guide: How to Choose

  • Check piece size for your kids' ages

    Smaller magnetic pieces are choking hazards for toddlers, so match the set to your youngest player.

  • Look for UV-resistant plastic

    Cheap pool toys fade and crack fast in direct sun after just a few weeks outdoors.

  • Confirm it floats and stores easily

    A mesh bag or collapsible design saves you from fishing toys out of the pool skimmer every night.
