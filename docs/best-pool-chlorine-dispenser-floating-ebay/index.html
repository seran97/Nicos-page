Pool Chlorine Dispenser Floating Buying Guide: 3 Steps

What to check before you toss a new dispenser in the water.

Confirm Your Tablet Size

Floating dispensers are usually built for either 1-inch or 3-inch tablets, not both. Check what size chlorine you already buy before ordering so you're not stuck with a mismatched holder.

Look at the Vent Adjustment Mechanism

The whole point of a dispenser is controlling how fast chlorine releases. Cheaper models have vents that drift open or closed on their own; better ones lock into a chosen setting so your chemical balance stays predictable.

Check Reviews for Sun Durability

Floating in direct sunlight all summer is rough on plastic. Look for buyers specifically mentioning a second or third season of use without cracking, not just first-week impressions.
