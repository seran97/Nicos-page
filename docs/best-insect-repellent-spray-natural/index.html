Insect Repellent Spray Natural Buying Guide: 3 Steps

Before you buy a natural repellent spray, run through these three checks to avoid disappointment on the trail.

Match the Formula to Your Activity

Backyard hangouts need less strength than deep-woods hikes. Pick strength accordingly rather than assuming one formula covers everything.

Patch Test Before Regular Use

Even natural ingredients like lavender oil can irritate sensitive skin, so test on a small area before applying head to toe.

Plan Your Reapplication Schedule

Natural sprays typically need reapplying every 2-3 hours for consistent coverage — pack the bottle, not just a single spritz.
