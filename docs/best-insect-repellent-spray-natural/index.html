Frequently Asked Questions

Is this spray safe to use on kids?

Being DEET-free and lavender-based, it's a popular choice for families, though we'd still recommend a small patch test on sensitive skin first.

How long does the protection last per application?

Most users reapply every 2-3 hours for consistent coverage, a bit more often than DEET-based sprays but comparable to other natural formulas.

Does it leave a strong scent on clothing?

It has a noticeable lavender fragrance while wet that fades to a light scent once dry, which most reviewers describe as pleasant rather than overpowering.

Is it effective against ticks as well as mosquitoes?

Reviews suggest it holds up reasonably well against mosquitoes, but for high tick-risk areas like tall grass or wooded trails, pairing it with permethrin-treated clothing is safer.

Can it be used on outdoor gear like tents or hammocks?

Yes, it's safe to mist directly on fabric gear since it's plant-based, though always check for any discoloration on delicate materials first.
