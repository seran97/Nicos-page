Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a separate app to view footage?

The dashcam connects via built-in WiFi to a companion app, letting you view, download, and share clips directly from your phone.

How good is the night vision quality?

The night vision mode noticeably improves clarity in low light, capturing license plates and road details that a standard sensor would blur out.

Does it record automatically when I start driving?

Yes, it powers on and starts recording automatically once your car's ignition triggers power to the unit.

What triggers the G-sensor to save a clip?

Sudden impacts or hard braking trigger the G-sensor, which locks that specific clip so it isn't overwritten by the loop recording.

How big of a memory card do I need?

A 32GB to 128GB microSD card works well, giving you several hours to a couple of days of loop-recorded footage depending on resolution settings.
