Buying Guide

3 Steps to Choosing the Right Beach Bag With Cooler

  1. Decide How Long You Need Things Cold

    If you're heading out for a 2–3 hour beach session, a basic insulated compartment like the Bluboon's is more than enough — especially with a couple of ice packs. For all-day trips or camping, you may want a dedicated hard-sided cooler instead. Be honest about your use case before buying, because most soft cooler bags (including this one) are optimized for shorter outings.

  2. Think About What Else You're Carrying

    A beach bag with a cooler compartment needs to handle more than just drinks. Consider whether you need room for towels, a change of clothes, sunscreen, toys, or a book. The Bluboon's open mesh main compartment is generously sized for most day-trip needs.
