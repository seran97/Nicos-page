Buying Guide

3 Steps to Choosing the Right Hiking Backpack

  1. Match Capacity to Your Trip Length

    A 40L pack like the Maelstrom is the sweet spot for 1-3 day hikes and weekend travel. If you're doing day hikes only, a 20-25L is sufficient. For 4+ day backcountry trips, look at 55-65L options. Getting this wrong means either carrying dead weight or running out of space mid-trail.

  2. Prioritize Real Waterproofing Over "Water-Resistant"

    Marketing language matters here. "Water-resistant" usually means it can handle light drizzle. Look for packs that explicitly state waterproof materials AND include a rain cover — the Maelstrom does both. Check buyer reviews specifically mentioning rain performance, not just the product description.

  3. Check the Feature-to-Price Ratio

    Hydration compatibility, tripod straps, load compression, and a rain cover are features you'd normally pay $80-120 for. When a pack under $40 checks all those boxes and has 529 reviews averaging 4.6★, that's a signal worth paying attention to. Don't pay for a brand name when the specs and community feedback tell the real story.
