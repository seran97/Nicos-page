Buying Guide: 3 Steps to the Right Hiking Backpack

  1. Match Capacity to Your Trip Length

    A 40L pack like the Maelstrom is the sweet spot for day hikes, overnight trips, and travel carry-ons. Going shorter? A 20–25L pack works. Multi-day backcountry? Look at 50–65L. Getting the volume right prevents both overpacking and leaving essentials behind.

  2. Check for Weather Protection Features

    Water-resistant fabric is a baseline — but a dedicated rain cover (like the one included with the Maelstrom) is what actually keeps your gear dry in a real downpour. Don't assume a "waterproof" label means full submersion protection; it usually means splash-resistant at best.

  3. Verify Comfort Features Before You Buy

    Padded shoulder straps, a sternum strap, and load compression straps are non-negotiable for anything over 5 miles. The Maelstrom checks all three. If you're carrying camera gear or trekking poles, tripod attachment straps (also included here) save a ton of hassle on the trail.
