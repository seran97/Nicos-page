Buying Guide: 3 Steps to Choosing Yours

Check the frame material. HDPE or powder-coated aluminum outlasts basic resin or steel-tube frames that rust or crack.
Confirm recline positions and locking mechanism. More positions with a solid hinge lock means better comfort and less wobble over time.
Plan for storage or covers. Even weather-resistant furniture lasts longer with a cover during off-season months or storms.
