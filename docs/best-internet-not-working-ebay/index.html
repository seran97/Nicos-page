Frequently Asked Questions

What's the first thing to check when the internet stops working?

Check whether other devices in the house are also affected. If they are, the issue is likely with your router or ISP, not a single device.

Could my Wi-Fi channel be causing the dropouts?

Yes, overlapping channels from neighboring networks can cause slowdowns. Switching to a less congested channel in your router settings often helps.
