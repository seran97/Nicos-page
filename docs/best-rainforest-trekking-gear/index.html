Buying Guide

Prioritize breathability over full waterproofing. In humid climates, trapped sweat is often worse than getting a little wet from rain.
Pack for layering. Rainforest treks can swing between hot midday sun and cooler mornings — plan your gear accordingly.
Test gear before your trip. Wear everything on a shorter hike first to catch fit or chafing issues before you're deep in the jungle.
