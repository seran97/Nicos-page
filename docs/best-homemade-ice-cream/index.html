Buying Guide

Check the seal type. Look for a screw-top or snap-lock lid rather than a loose-fit cover to minimize freezer burn.
Match capacity to your maker. Most home ice cream makers produce 1.5 to 2 quarts per batch, so size accordingly.
Pre-chill before filling. Chilling the container beforehand helps the ice cream set faster and stay smoother.
