Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Q-MAX rating actually mean?

Q-MAX measures how quickly a material pulls heat away on contact; a rating above 0.5 is considered strong cooling performance for a fabric-based pet mat, noticeably cooler to the touch than an untreated blanket.

Is it sized for genuinely large dogs?

Yes, it's cut generously enough for large breeds like Labradors and German Shepherds to lie fully stretched out, not just curled up.

Can I put it in the washing machine?

Yes, it's machine washable on a gentle cycle, which matters for a large-breed mat that sees heavy daily use and shedding.

Does it work on hardwood floors or only carpet?

It works on any surface since the cooling effect comes from the material itself, though it can shift slightly on very smooth hardwood without a rug underneath.

How long does the cooling sensation last per session?

Most owners report a solid 15-20 minutes of noticeably cooler contact before it gradually equalizes, then resets again once the dog steps away for a few minutes.
