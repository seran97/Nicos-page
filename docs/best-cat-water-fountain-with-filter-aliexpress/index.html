Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I change the filter?

Most owners replace it every 2-4 weeks depending on water hardness and how many cats share it.

Is this fountain quiet enough for a bedroom?

Yes, the pump runs quietly enough for most bedrooms, though very silence-sensitive sleepers may still notice a faint hum.

Can I use tap water in it?

Yes, though filtered or softened water will extend the life of the internal filter.

Does it work for multiple cats?

It works for one or two cats comfortably; three or more may drain it faster than expected.

How do I clean the pump?

Unplug it, remove the pump housing, and rinse the impeller under running water weekly.
