Buying Guide

Measure your dog lying down. Pick a mat size that lets them fully stretch out, not just curl up.
Place it somewhere shaded. Direct sun can reduce how long the cooling gel stays effective.
Let it recharge between uses. A few minutes off the mat resets the cooling effect for next time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a cooling mat work without electricity or refrigeration?

It uses a pressure-activated cooling gel that absorbs your dog's body heat on contact, so it works passively without plugging in or freezing anything first.

Does it stay cool all day?

The cooling effect recharges automatically once your dog steps off for a bit, letting the gel reset, so it stays useful throughout a hot day rather than working only once.

Is it safe if my dog scratches or chews at it?

The leakproof design holds up to normal scratching and lying down, but determined chewers can puncture the surface, so supervise heavy chewers the first few uses.

Can I wash it if my dog has an accident?

Yes, the waterproof, non-slip surface wipes clean easily and can handle a damp cloth or gentle hand wash without damaging the internal cooling gel.

Will it work for a couch, not just the floor?

Yes, it doubles as a couch cover thanks to the waterproof, non-slip backing, which also protects furniture from fur, drool, and accidents.
