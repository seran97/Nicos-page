Buying Guide

  1. Check seller feedback percentage before buying
    A high positive feedback rating with a good number of reviews is the best signal of a reliable seller.
  2. Ask about the product's condition and age
    Cosmetics have shelf lives, so confirming the item isn't expired is worth a quick message.

    Sometimes discontinued items are priced higher due to scarcity, so weigh the premium against how much you want that exact shade.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a genuine, authentic product?

Authenticity depends on the individual seller; checking their feedback rating and reviews is the best way to verify before purchase.

Why isn't it sold with the original box?

Many resale cosmetic listings are sold without retail packaging, often because they're discontinued or from clearance stock.

Can I pick my exact shade?

Yes, listings typically let you choose your specific shade number from what the seller has in stock.

Is the product expired?

Reputable sellers note the condition and approximate age in their listing; message them directly if it's not specified.

What if the shade doesn't match what I expected?

Check the seller's return policy before ordering, as this can vary between individual marketplace sellers.
