Does stainless steel rust over time outdoors?

Quality stainless resists rust well, though owners recommend drying tools before storage rather than leaving them exposed to rain repeatedly.

Is the case stainless steel too, or just the tools?

Just the tools — the included case is typically a fabric or plastic carrying case, not metal.

How does the weight compare to aluminum grill tools?

Stainless is noticeably heavier, which most reviewers see as a plus for stability when flipping heavier cuts of meat.
