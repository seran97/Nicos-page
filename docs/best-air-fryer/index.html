How to choose an air fryer in 4 questions

1. How many people do you cook for?

As a rough guide: 2 quarts for one person or snacks, 4 to 5 quarts for two to four people, 6 quarts or more, or a dual-basket model, for families and batch cooking.

2. Basket or oven style?

Basket air fryers crisp faster and are easier to clean. Oven-style models with racks fit more flat food (toast, pizza) but usually crisp less evenly.

3. One basket or two?

Two baskets let you cook a main and a side at different temperatures and finish them together. They take more counter space and cost more.

4. What about the coating?

Most baskets are nonstick. If you prefer to avoid PFAS-based coatings, look for ceramic baskets such as the COSORI Pro LE, and always use wooden or silicone tools.

Air fryer tips that make a real difference

  • Do not overfill the basket: a single layer crisps; a pile steams.
  • Shake or flip halfway through cooking.
  • Use a light spray or brush of oil for browning; skip aerosol sprays that can damage nonstick coatings.
  • Preheat 3 to 5 minutes for frozen foods.
  • Clean the basket after each use so grease does not smoke next time.

Frequently asked questions

What size air fryer do I need?

About 2 quarts for one person, 4 to 5 quarts for two to four people and 6 quarts or a dual-basket model for families or batch cooking.

Is an air fryer healthier than deep frying?

It uses far less oil, so most fried foods have less fat and fewer calories when air-fried. It is still a way of cooking, not a diet: what you cook matters most.

Do air fryers use a lot of electricity?

Most draw about 1,200 to 1,700 W, but they cook faster than an oven and heat a small space, so a typical session often uses less energy than heating a full oven.

Can I put aluminum foil or parchment in an air fryer?

Yes, if it is weighed down by food and does not block the airflow. Perforated parchment liners made for air fryers are the safer option.

Single or dual basket?

Choose dual if you often cook a main and a side together. Choose single if you mostly cook one food or have little counter space.
