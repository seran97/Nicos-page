Buying Guide

Check if your pet tolerates touch on all areas. Gloves work best when your pet is comfortable being touched broadly — for very touch-sensitive pets, start with short sessions.
Match glove size to your hand. A too-large glove loses grip and control; check the sizing chart rather than assuming one-size-fits-all.
Groom during, not after, shedding season peaks. Regular light sessions during peak shedding prevent the mats and tumbleweeds that build up if you wait too long between grooming.
