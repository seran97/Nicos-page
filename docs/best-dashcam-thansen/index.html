Frequently Asked Questions

Why would I need three cameras instead of two?

A third interior-facing camera documents cabin activity, useful if you drive rideshare or want proof of what happened inside the car during a dispute, not just outside.

Does the interior camera work at night?

Yes, it uses infrared to capture usable interior footage with the cabin lights off, though image quality is grayscale in low light rather than full color.

How much storage do I need for three channels?

The included 64GB card covers roughly 8-10 hours of triple-channel footage before looping, enough for most daily driving without manual management.

Does the WiFi drain my phone's battery faster?

Not meaningfully — the WiFi only activates when you open the app to review or export footage, so it doesn't run continuously in the background.

Can I disable the interior camera for privacy?

Yes, the interior channel can be turned off independently in settings while front and rear recording stay active.
