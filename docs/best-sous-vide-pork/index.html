Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really overcook pork with sous vide?

It's much harder than with a stovetop — the water bath holds an exact temperature, so pork stays at the doneness you set for hours without drying out, unlike a hot pan.

What temperature is best for pork tenderloin?

Most home cooks land around 140-145°F for tender, juicy tenderloin. The 14 built-in presets on this cooker include pork-specific settings so you don't have to look it up.

Do I need special bags for sous vide pork?

Any BPA-free freezer-safe zip bag works fine using the water displacement method, though vacuum-sealed bags give slightly more consistent results for longer cooks.
