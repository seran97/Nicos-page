  1. Look for air-core or reinforced fill, not just gel. This matters most if your dog drops onto surfaces heavily rather than lying down gently.
  2. Pick your spot deliberately. A shaded floor area or couch cushion both work, but avoid direct sun exposure for best cooling performance.
  3. Spot-clean between full washes. A quick wipe-down after muddy paws extends time between deeper machine washes.
