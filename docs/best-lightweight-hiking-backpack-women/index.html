Buying Guide

  1. Measure your torso, not just pick a size online
    A pack that's technically 'unisex' can still ride oddly on a smaller frame — check strap adjustability specifically.
  2. Decide if you need a hydration sleeve
    If you plan on longer hikes, prioritize a pack with a hydration bladder pocket even if it's sold separately.
  3. Test the fold-down feature before a trip
    Practice packing it flat once at home so you're not fumbling with it during travel.
