Keep Cat Entertained Buying Guide: 3 Steps

Before buying an enrichment toy, check these three things to make sure your cat will actually engage with it long term.

Consider Your Cat's Play Style

Cats that stalk and pounce tend to respond well to wearable, hand-driven toys like this one, since the movement mimics real prey more closely than a static toy.

Check Durability Against Claws and Teeth

Look for reinforced stitching or a protective sleeve if your cat plays rough, since flimsy toys can tear within the first few sessions.

Rotate Toys to Keep Interest High

Even a great toy loses novelty over time. Rotating it with one or two others every couple weeks keeps engagement higher long term.
