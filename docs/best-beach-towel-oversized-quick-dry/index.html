Buying Guide: 3 Steps to the Right Beach Towel

  1. Check the Actual Dimensions

    Not all "oversized" towels are created equal. A true oversized beach towel should be at least 70″ long — anything shorter and you'll have your feet hanging off the edge. The Genovega set hits 74″ × 38″, which is genuinely large enough for most adults to lie on fully without folding. Always verify the listed dimensions before buying, especially with budget sets that use "oversized" loosely in their marketing.

  2. Prioritize Weave Type for Your Use Case

    Terry cloth is plush and absorbent but slow to dry and a sand magnet. Turkish flat-weave (like this set) dries fast, sheds sand easily, and packs down small — ideal for beach days, travel, and camping. If you want a spa-like experience at home and don't care about drying time, terry is fine. But for active outdoor use, flat-weave wins every time.

  3. Calculate Cost Per Towel, Not Just Total Price

    A $44 price tag sounds like a lot until you realize it's 6 towels — that's under $7.50 each. Compare that to buying individual towels at $12–$18 a piece and the math becomes obvious fast.
