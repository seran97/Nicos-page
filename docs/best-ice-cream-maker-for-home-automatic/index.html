Buying Guide

Pre-freeze the bowl overnight. Plan at least 8-12 hours ahead of when you want to churn.
Chill your mixture before churning. A cold base freezes faster and yields a creamier texture.
Store the bowl in the freezer permanently. Keeping it frozen year-round means you're always ready for a spontaneous batch.

FAQs

Does this machine make gelato and sorbet too?

Yes, this model handles gelato, frozen yogurt, and sorbet in addition to standard ice cream, using the same freezer-bowl churning process.

How long does a 2-quart batch take?

Most batches finish churning in 20-40 minutes depending on the recipe's fat content and how well-chilled the mixture is beforehand.

Do I need to pre-freeze anything?

Yes, the mixing bowl needs to be frozen solid for at least 8-12 hours before churning, so plan ahead the night before.

Is the mixing bowl dishwasher safe?

Most owners hand wash the bowl to protect the coating, though some report light dishwasher use on the top rack without issues.

Can I double a batch in this machine?

No, the 2-quart bowl capacity is fixed, so doubling a recipe requires running two separate batches.
