How To Choose: 3 Steps

Decide how much structure you need

Empire waist and loose silhouettes prioritize comfort over shape — if you want more definition, look for a fitted-waist alternative instead.

Check the strap width and adjustability

Thin spaghetti straps look great but may need a strapless bra or bralette for more support during long wear.

Hang it up as soon as it arrives

Lightweight maxi fabrics wrinkle fast in packaging — hanging it immediately saves you an ironing session before wearing it out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an empire waist maxi dress flattering for all body types?

Empire waists are generally considered one of the more universally flattering cuts since they skim over the midsection rather than fitting tightly to it.

Can I wear a casual maxi dress to work?

It depends on your office's dress code, but a solid-color casual maxi paired with a blazer or cardigan can work for business-casual settings.

What's the general consensus on r/deals about maxi dresses?

Loose, comfortable maxi dresses in the $20-$50 range are a frequently recommended category on r/deals for anyone wanting an easy summer staple without a big investment.

Do spaghetti strap dresses need a special bra?

A strapless or convertible bra usually works best, since regular bra straps tend to peek out from thin spaghetti straps.
