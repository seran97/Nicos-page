Frequently Asked Questions

Is this dishwasher safe?

Yes, most collapsible silicone bowls are top-rack dishwasher safe, but check the specific listing.

How much water does it hold when fully open?

Typically around 1.5-2 cups depending on the exact model size.

Will it tip over easily?

It's more stable than a shallow dish, but very active dogs may still knock it over on uneven ground.

Can I use it for food too?

Yes, most sets include a separate compartment or second bowl for food.

Does it smell after use?

Rinse promptly after each use; letting food or water sit can cause odor buildup over time.
