Buying Guide

Check the size against your group. 80x80 inches works for 6-8 people; if it's just a couple, a smaller blanket packs lighter.
Confirm the backing material. Look for a sealed waterproof layer rather than a "water-resistant" coating that only repels light moisture.
Think about storage. If you'll carry it far, prioritize one with a built-in strap or pouch over one that just folds loose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this picnic blanket actually waterproof or just water-resistant?

It has a fully sealed backing layer, so it blocks moisture from wet grass or damp sand rather than just beading water off the surface like thinner mats.

How many people can comfortably sit on it?

At 80x80 inches, it comfortably seats 6-8 adults sitting cross-legged, or 4-5 if you want space to spread out food and bags.

Can I machine wash it after a messy picnic?

Yes, it's machine washable on a gentle cold cycle. Just skip the dryer's high heat setting and let it air dry to protect the waterproof coating.

Does it fold down small enough for a backpack?

It folds into a compact square with an attached carry strap, so it fits in a daypack or under a stroller without much bulk.

Will it slide around on a windy beach?

The corners have loops for stakes or sandbags, which helps a lot in wind, though on very gusty days weighting the edges with shoes or bags still helps.
