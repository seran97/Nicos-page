Frequently Asked Questions

Why would internet keep dropping in certain rooms?

Wi-Fi signals weaken through walls and distance, so rooms far from the router often lose connection even when the main line is fine. A wired backbone like coax or ethernet solves this at the source instead of just boosting a weak signal.

What is a MoCA adapter and how does it help?

MoCA adapters turn your home's existing coax cable wiring into a wired ethernet backbone, giving you a stable, high-speed connection to rooms that Wi-Fi struggles to reach.

Do I need special coax wiring already installed?

Most homes already have coax outlets from prior cable TV installations, and this adapter works with that existing wiring without new cable runs.

Will this conflict with my cable internet service?

No, MoCA operates on a different frequency band than cable internet, so it runs alongside your existing service without interference.

Is setup complicated for someone who isn't technical?

Setup is mostly plug-and-play, connect the adapters to existing coax outlets and your router or device, and most users are online within 15-20 minutes.
