Frequently Asked Questions

How does a paw washer cup work?

You fill the cup with water, dip and gently twist your dog's paw inside, and the soft bristles clean off mud and debris.

Is it suitable for large dog breeds?

It works best for small to medium breeds, though it can handle larger paws with a bit more care.

Do I need soap in the cup?

Plain water is usually enough for everyday mud and debris, though a small amount of pet-safe soap can help with heavier grime.

Is it easy to travel with?

Yes, its compact portable design makes it easy to bring along on hikes or trips.

How do I clean the cup itself after use?

Simply rinse it out with water after each use, and it dries quickly for the next walk.
