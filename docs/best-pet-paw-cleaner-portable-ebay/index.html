Keep it filled and ready by the door. Having water pre-filled and waiting means less delay between coming inside and getting paws clean.

How does the paw cleaner actually work?

You fill the cup partway with water, dunk each paw in, and gently twist — the soft internal bristles work mud and debris loose without needing a separate towel or hose.

It comfortably fits small to medium paw sizes; for large or giant breeds, double-check the cup diameter against your dog's paw before ordering.

Do I need to refill the water for every paw?

Not necessarily for lightly muddy paws, but for heavily soiled ones, refreshing the water partway through keeps each paw actually getting clean rather than dirtier.
