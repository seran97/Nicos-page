Frequently Asked Questions

Is IP66 waterproof enough for outdoor use year-round?

Yes, IP66 means it's protected against powerful water jets from any direction, which covers rain, sprinklers, and snow. Just avoid full submersion.

Do I need a hub for this smart plug?

No, most outdoor waterproof smart plugs connect directly to your home WiFi and are controlled through a companion app, no separate hub required.

Can it handle high-wattage devices like string lights or a pressure washer?

Check the plug's amp rating before use. Most outdoor smart plugs handle up to 15A, which is fine for lights and small appliances but not heavy-duty power tools.

Will it work with Alexa and Google Home?

Most current-generation outdoor smart plugs support both Alexa and Google Assistant voice control alongside the dedicated app.

How does it hold up in freezing winters?

IP66-rated housings are built to handle temperature swings and moisture, but always check the manufacturer's operating temperature range if you're in an extreme climate.
