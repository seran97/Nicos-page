Buying Guide: 3 Steps to Choose the Right Outdoor Smart Plug

Check the IP Waterproof Rating First

Not all outdoor smart plugs are created equal. Look for at least IP65 for covered outdoor areas, but for fully exposed locations — patios, garden beds, driveways — you need IP66 or higher. The ELEGRP PQR22's IP66 rating means it can handle powerful water jets from any direction, making it the gold standard for exposed outdoor use.

Decide How Many Devices You Need to Control

Single-socket smart plugs are fine for one device, but if you're managing string lights AND a fountain, or two separate garden light zones, a dual-socket plug like the PQR22 saves money and reduces outlet clutter. The key differentiator here is independent control — make sure each socket can be switched and dimmed separately, not just as a pair.

Confirm Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

Before buying, verify the plug works with your existing smart home setup. The ELEGRP PQR22 supports Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, covering the vast majority of smart home users. Also confirm your router broadcasts a 2.4GHz WiFi band, as most outdoor smart plugs — including this one — require it for reliable connectivity at longer outdoor distances.
