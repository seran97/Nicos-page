How to choose a patio mosquito repellent

Check the coverage radius. Match the device's stated range to the actual size of your patio or seating area.
Consider battery life. Rechargeable units save money over disposable refills, but confirm run-time matches your typical evening length.
Think about placement. Portable units let you reposition for wind direction or move between the patio and indoors as needed.
