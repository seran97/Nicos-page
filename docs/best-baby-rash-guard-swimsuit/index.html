  1. Check the Size Chart Before Ordering

    Baby swimwear sizing varies significantly between brands. Measure your baby's height and weight and compare against the specific size chart on the Amazon listing. When in doubt, size up — a slightly larger fit is far more comfortable and gives room to grow through the season.

  2. Prioritize Sun Coverage for Your Activity

    If your baby will be spending extended time outdoors at the beach, pool, or splash pad, long-sleeve rash guards are a must. This one-piece design covers the torso and arms, reducing the amount of sunscreen you need to apply and reapply — a genuine time-saver with a wriggly baby.
