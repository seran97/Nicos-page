Frequently Asked Questions

Does tofu litter track less than clay litter?

Reviewers consistently report less tracking than clay because the granules are lighter and less powdery, though some dust still escapes during the first few uses of a new bag.

How does it handle odor compared to clumping clay?

The scented tofu formula does a solid job masking ammonia smell for 2-3 days per scoop cycle, though homes with multiple cats may need daily scooping regardless of litter type.

Is it safe if my cat eats a little by accident?

Tofu-based litter is made from soybean pulp and is considered non-toxic in small amounts, which is part of why many owners with curious kittens prefer it over clay.

Can it be flushed down the toilet?

Small clumps can typically be flushed in modest amounts, but check your local plumbing guidelines first since septic systems vary in what they can handle.

How long does one 5.5lb bag actually last?

For a single cat with a standard litter box, most buyers report roughly 3-4 weeks per bag with daily scooping, though multi-cat households go through it noticeably faster.
