Frequently Asked Questions

Does tofu litter track less than clay litter?

Reviewers consistently report less tracking than clay because the granules are lighter and less powdery, though some dust still escapes during the first few uses of a new bag.

How does it handle odor compared to clumping clay?

The scented tofu formula does a solid job masking ammonia smell for 2-3 days per scoop cycle, though multi-cat homes may need daily scooping regardless of litter type.

Is it safe if my cat eats a little by accident?

Tofu-based litter is made from soybean pulp and is considered non-toxic in small amounts, which is part of why owners with curious kittens tend to prefer it over clay.
