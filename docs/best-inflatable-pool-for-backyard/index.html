How long does it take to set up?

At 5.5ft across, it comfortably fits 2-3 kids splashing around, or a couple of adults sitting and cooling off.

The thickened base resists most everyday wear, though a ground tarp underneath is recommended on rougher surfaces like patios or gravel.

Most models include a drain valve near the base so you can empty it directly onto the lawn without needing to tip the whole pool.
