Running shoes are the gift runners actually use every week, but only if the size and the type of running fit. A cushioned road shoe, a trail shoe and a lightweight trainer feel very different. Each pick below says who it suits and links to our full review; prices and sizes change fast in December, so check the store before buying.

  • Ask (or check their current pair) for the exact size and width; running shoe sizes vary between brands.
  • Road shoes are for pavement and treadmills; trail shoes have more grip and protection for dirt and rocks.
  • Buy from a store with free returns or keep the gift receipt, so they can swap the size after Christmas.
