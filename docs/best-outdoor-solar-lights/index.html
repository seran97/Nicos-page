Frequently Asked Questions

How long do these solar lights stay on at night?

With a full day of direct sun, most units run 8-10 hours, easily covering dusk to midnight in most seasons.

Do they still work on cloudy or rainy days?

Yes, though runtime drops to roughly 4-6 hours on overcast days since the panel gets less charge — placement in the most sun-exposed spot helps a lot.

Is the IP65 rating enough for heavy rain?

IP65 means fully dust-protected and resistant to low-pressure water jets, which covers rain, sprinklers and snow without issue.

Do the stakes work in rocky or hard soil?

The included ground stakes handle average garden soil well, but very rocky or compacted ground may need a pilot hole with a screwdriver first.

Can I replace the built-in batteries later?

Yes, each unit uses a standard rechargeable NiMH battery accessible from the top panel, so you can swap it after a couple of years of use.
