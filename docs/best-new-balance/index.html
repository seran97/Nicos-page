Frequently Asked Questions

Do New Balance shoes tend to run true to size?

Most reviewers find New Balance runs close to true to size, though the brand is known for a slightly wider toe box compared to some competitors, so half-size-down buyers with narrow feet may want to size accordingly.

Are these good for all-day wear, not just workouts?

Yes, many buyers use them as everyday walking and casual shoes thanks to the cushioned midsole, not exclusively for training sessions.

How long does the cushioning typically last before breaking down?

Reviewers commonly report solid support for 300 to 500 miles of regular wear before noticing a drop in cushioning response.

Is the outsole grip reliable on wet pavement?

The rubber outsole provides decent traction in light rain, though like most sneakers it's not designed for very slick or icy conditions.

Do they come in wide width options?

New Balance is well known for offering wide and extra-wide width options across many of its models, more so than most competing brands.
