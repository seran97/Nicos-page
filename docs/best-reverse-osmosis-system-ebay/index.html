Reverse Osmosis System Buying Guide: 3 Steps

A quick framework before you commit to a purchase.

Confirm Your Housing Size First

Before ordering, measure your current membrane housing or check your system's manual. Most standard under-sink RO systems use a common form factor, but a mismatch means wasted money and a leaky install.

Check Your Water Hardness

If your water is especially hard or high in sediment, consider a pre-filter upgrade alongside the membrane swap — it extends the life of the replacement significantly and keeps your flow rate consistent.
