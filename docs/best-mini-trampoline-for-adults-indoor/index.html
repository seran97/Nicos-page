How to choose the right mini trampoline

Check the weight capacity. Match the rated max load to your own weight with some margin for bouncing dynamics, not just standing weight.
Consider noise and floor space. Bungee suspension tends to run quieter than metal springs, which matters if you live in an apartment.
Look for foldability. If storage is tight, prioritize models that fold flat or collapse for under-bed storage.
