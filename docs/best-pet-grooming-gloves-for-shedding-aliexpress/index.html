Buying Guide

Start with short sessions if your pet is skittish. A few minutes at a time builds trust faster than one long forced session.
Rotate the two gloves between washes. Keeping one clean and dry while the other is in the wash keeps grooming sessions consistent.
Check bristle stiffness against your pet's coat type. Thicker double coats tolerate firmer bristles better than sensitive, thin-coated breeds.
