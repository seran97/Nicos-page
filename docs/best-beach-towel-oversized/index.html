How to Buy the Right Oversized Beach Towel

Decide on Size & Coverage

Standard beach towels (27×54 in) feel cramped on sand. Look for at least 36×72 inches for comfortable full-body coverage. If you're tall or like to spread out, go even bigger. The Salt Earth set hits the sweet spot at 36×72 without being unwieldy to carry.

What makes Turkish cotton beach towels different? +
Turkish cotton has longer fibers than standard cotton, making towels softer, more absorbent, and quicker to dry. It also tends to be lighter, so it's easier to pack for beach trips. The flat-weave construction is a bonus — it resists sand clinging far better than looped terry cloth.
Is the Salt Earth towel really sand resistant? +
Yes — the flat-weave construction means sand doesn't cling the way it does with looped terry cloth. A quick shake removes most sand before you pack up. Multiple reviewers on Amazon specifically called this out as one of the biggest practical benefits over their old towels.
How do I care for these towels? +
Machine wash cold, tumble dry low. Avoid fabric softener as it can reduce absorbency over time. They get softer with each wash — a common trait of Turkish cotton that buyers on Amazon frequently mention in positive reviews after a few months of use.
