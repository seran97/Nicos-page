Buying Guide

Measure your umbrella rib or pole thickness. Clip-on lights have a maximum grip width — check the listing specs against your umbrella before ordering.
Prioritize the IP65 rating. Anything lower than IP65 risks water getting into the solar panel or battery compartment during a summer storm.
Place it where it gets direct sun. Even a partially shaded umbrella can block enough sun to slow the solar charge — clip it near the top edge of the canopy for best results.
