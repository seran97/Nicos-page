Buying Guide: What To Look For

  1. Measure your child's face before ordering

    Full-face masks rely on a proper seal to work safely — check the size chart against your child's actual face measurements, not just age range.

  2. Check for anti-fog and dry-top snorkel features

    A dry-top valve keeps water out if the mask dips below the surface, and anti-fog coating keeps visibility clear.

  3. Prioritize safety certifications and reviews on breathing ease

    Look for reviews specifically mentioning easy, unrestricted breathing, since some full-face masks have had CO2 buildup concerns in cheaper models.
