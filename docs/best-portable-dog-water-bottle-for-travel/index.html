3-Step Buying Guide

Follow these steps before purchasing any portable dog water bottle for travel to make sure you get the right fit.

Match Capacity to Your Dog's Size & Trip Length

A 27 oz bottle like the LumoLeaf is ideal for small-to-medium dogs (under 50 lbs) on walks or day trips up to 2–3 hours. For larger breeds or longer hikes, consider whether you'll have refill access along the route. As a rule of thumb, dogs need about 1 oz of water per pound of body weight per day — plan accordingly.

Prioritize Insulation If You Travel in Warm Climates

Stainless steel insulation isn't just a luxury — in hot weather, warm water can discourage dogs from drinking, increasing dehydration risk. The LumoLeaf's double-wall construction keeps water cool for hours, making it a practical choice for summer hikes, beach trips, or travel in warmer regions. If you only walk in cool weather, a lighter plastic option may suffice.
