Buying Guide: How to Choose

  • Prioritize collapsible or packable designs

  • Rinse and dry fully before folding

    Sand left in the folding joints can cause grit buildup that makes future collapsing harder.

  • Check for a mesh bag or storage pouch

    Sand toys are notorious for scattering everywhere, so built-in storage is worth prioritizing.
