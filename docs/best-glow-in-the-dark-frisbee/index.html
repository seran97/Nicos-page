Buying Guide: What To Look For

  1. Look for LED discs over glow-paint discs

    LED discs stay bright for hours regardless of ambient light, while glow-paint discs need direct light exposure and fade quickly.

  2. Check if it's rechargeable or battery-replaceable

    USB rechargeable options save money long-term versus discs that need disposable batteries swapped out constantly.

  3. Consider a 2-pack for group play

    A single disc limits play to one game at a time; a pack lets kids or groups play multiple games simultaneously.
