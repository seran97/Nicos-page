Check the Foam Thickness and Diameter

Thin, cheap noodles snap or split within weeks of active play. Look for descriptions mentioning extra-thick or reinforced foam if kids (or adults) will be roughhousing with them.

Extra-large 52-inch noodles are great for taller kids and adults who want more support, but may feel unwieldy in smaller above-ground pools. Match the length to how the noodle will actually be used.
