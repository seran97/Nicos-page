Buying Guide

3 Steps to Choosing the Right Air Purifier

Not sure if this is the right pick for your situation? Follow these three steps before buying any air purifier.

  1. Measure your room size first. Air purifiers are rated by CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) and recommended room coverage. The Momcozy is ideal for bedrooms and nurseries up to roughly 215–300 sq ft. If your space is larger, look for a unit with a higher CADR rating. Don't overspend on a large-room unit if you only need it for a bedroom.
  2. Decide which pollutants matter most to you. If you have allergies or a baby, H13 HEPA is the gold standard — it captures pollen, dust mites, pet dander, and fine particulates. If cooking odors or VOCs are your concern, make sure the unit includes an activated carbon layer. The Momcozy's 4-in-1 filter covers both bases, which is why it scores so well with families.
  3. Check ongoing filter costs before committing. The purchase price is only part of the equation. Factor in replacement filter costs every 3–6 months. The Momcozy's replacement filters are reasonably priced on Amazon, and the built-in indicator means you replace them only when needed — not on a guesswork schedule.
