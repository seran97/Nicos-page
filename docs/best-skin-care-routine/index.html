Frequently Asked Questions

What order should I use a skin care routine set in?

Generally cleanser first, then toner, then vitamin C serum, and finish with moisturizer. Serums go on clean, slightly damp skin so they absorb better, and moisturizer seals everything in.

Is a 6-piece set overkill for beginners?

Not really. Starting with a matched set removes the guesswork of picking individual products that might not play well together, which is the most common beginner mistake.

Can I use this routine if I have sensitive skin?

Niacinamide and vitamin C are generally well tolerated, but always patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours before applying a new product to your face, especially if you have reactive skin.

How soon will I see results?

Most people notice a brighter, more even tone within 2-4 weeks of consistent use. Deeper changes like texture and fine lines can take 8-12 weeks.

Do I still need sunscreen with this routine?

Yes, always. Vitamin C and niacinamide can make skin slightly more sun-aware, so a broad-spectrum SPF during the day is non-negotiable.
