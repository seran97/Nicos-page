The 4-step routine (morning and night)

1. Cleanse

Use a gentle cleanser morning and night. Squeaky-clean skin means the cleanser is too harsh. In the morning, dry or sensitive skin can simply rinse with water.

2. Treat (optional, one active at a time)

This is where a serum goes: niacinamide or BHA for oil and pores, vitamin C for dullness, retinol at night for texture and fine lines. See our face serum guide to pick one.

3. Moisturize

Every skin type needs a moisturizer, including oily skin. Gel textures suit oily skin; creams with ceramides suit dry skin.

4. Protect (morning)

Finish every morning with broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. It is the step with the strongest evidence against early aging and dark spots. Compare options in our SPF 50 face sunscreen guide.

Routine by skin type

Oily or acne-prone: foaming cleanser, niacinamide or 2% BHA (at night, a few times a week), water-gel moisturizer, oil-free sunscreen.

Dry: hydrating cleanser at night and water in the morning, hyaluronic acid on damp skin, ceramide cream, a moisturizing sunscreen.

Sensitive: minimalist cleanser, skip actives for the first two weeks, fragrance-free moisturizer, mineral sunscreen. Add one active at a time.

Combination: gentle cleanser, light gel moisturizer all over and a richer cream only on dry areas, oil-free sunscreen.

Mistakes that ruin a good routine

  • Using several strong actives at once (retinol, acids and vitamin C on the same night).
  • Skipping moisturizer because the skin is oily.
  • Applying too little sunscreen, or none on cloudy days.
  • Changing products every week. Give each product 6 to 12 weeks.

Frequently asked questions

What is the correct order of a skin care routine?

From thinnest to thickest: cleanser, toner if you use one, serum, moisturizer and, in the morning, sunscreen last.

What is the simplest skin care routine?

Cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen in the morning; cleanser and moisturizer at night. Add a serum only for a specific goal.

Do I need a different routine at night?

Yes. Skip sunscreen at night and use that time for retinol or exfoliating acids, which work better without sun exposure.

How long before a new routine shows results?

Hydration improves within days. Breakouts and texture usually take 4 to 8 weeks, dark spots about 12 weeks.

Are drugstore products good enough?

Yes. The cleansers, moisturizers and sunscreens in this guide are drugstore or mid-priced and are among the most rated in their category.
