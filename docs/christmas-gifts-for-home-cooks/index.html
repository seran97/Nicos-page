Christmas Gifts for Home Cooks: Air Fryers and Kitchen Upgrades (2026)

✓ 7 gift ideas ✓ Full review for each pick ✓ No paid placements

An air fryer is one of the few kitchen gifts that gets used every week: it reheats, roasts and crisps faster than an oven. The trick is the size. A 2-quart model is perfect for one person and useless for a family of five, so each pick below says how many people it feeds.

  • Rough sizing: 2 qt for one person, 4 to 5 qt for two or three, 6 qt or a dual basket for families.
  • Check counter space: larger and dual-basket models are wide.
  • If they already own an air fryer, a smart countertop oven or a cold brew maker is a better upgrade.
