Frequently Asked Questions

Do plant-based mosquito repellent sticks actually work outdoors?
Yes, when used in a somewhat enclosed patio area without heavy wind. The rosemary, lemongrass, and citronella smoke creates a barrier mosquitoes tend to avoid, though open windy yards need more sticks or a windbreak.
How long does one incense stick last?
Most buyers report 45 to 90 minutes of burn time per stick, so for a longer evening you'll want to light a fresh one roughly every hour.
Is this safe to use around pets and kids?
The formula is plant-based rather than DEET-based, which many buyers prefer for yards with children and dogs, but lit sticks should stay out of direct reach in a ventilated area.
Will it get rid of an existing mosquito infestation?
It's designed as a deterrent for active outdoor time, not a full extermination solution — pair it with removing standing water for better long-term results.
