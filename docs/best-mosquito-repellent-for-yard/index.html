Frequently Asked Questions

Do plant-based mosquito repellent sticks actually work outdoors?

Yes, when used in a somewhat enclosed patio area without heavy wind. The rosemary, lemongrass and citronella smoke creates a barrier that mosquitoes avoid, though open windy yards will need more sticks or a windbreak.

How long does one incense stick last?

Most users report 45 to 90 minutes of burn time per stick, so for an evening gathering you'll want to light a fresh one roughly every hour.

Is this safe to use around pets and kids?

The formula is plant-based rather than DEET-based, which many buyers prefer for backyards with children and dogs, but you should still keep lit sticks out of direct reach and ensure the area is ventilated.

Will it get rid of an existing mosquito infestation?

It's designed as a deterrent for active outdoor time, not a full extermination solution. Pair it with removing standing water sources for best long-term results.

How does this compare to citronella candles?

Incense sticks tend to produce a more directional, portable smoke barrier and are easier to move between seating areas compared to a stationary candle.
