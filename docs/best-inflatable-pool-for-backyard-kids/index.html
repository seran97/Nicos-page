Frequently Asked Questions

What age range is this pool best suited for?

It works well for toddlers through pre-teens, and the 22-inch depth is shallow enough for parents to feel comfortable supervising younger kids splashing around.

Does it come with a repair kit for punctures?

Yes, most family-size units like this include a basic vinyl patch kit in the box, which handles small punctures from twigs or toys.

How many kids can comfortably use it at once?

Comfortably 3-4 kids under 10, though it can technically hold more for shorter bursts of play.

Is the thickened liner safe on rougher ground like a stone patio?

The thickened PVC handles smooth patios fine, but we'd still recommend a tarp or foam mat underneath on any rougher gravel or stone surface.

How do I store it when the season ends?

Deflate fully, let it air-dry completely to prevent mildew, then fold loosely into a bin rather than a tight roll to avoid seam stress.
