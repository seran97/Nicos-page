Frequently Asked Questions

Does this foldable trampoline really fold flat?

Yes, the frame is designed to fold in half for storage, which makes it easy to slide behind a couch or into a closet.

Is buying a fitness item on eBay safe?

eBay's buyer protection covers items that don't arrive or aren't as described, and most sellers of fitness gear maintain strong feedback ratings you can check before purchasing.

How much floor space does the 40-inch trampoline need?

Plan for roughly a 4x4 foot clearance including bounce room around the edges, plus overhead clearance if you have low ceilings.

Can I return it if the fit isn't right for my space?

Most eBay sellers offer a return window, typically 30 days - check the specific listing's return policy before buying to confirm.

Is assembly required out of the box?

Yes, basic assembly of the legs and frame is required, generally taking about 15-20 minutes with the included tools.
