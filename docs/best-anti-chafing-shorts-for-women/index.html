Buying Guide: 3 Steps to the Right Pick

Get Your Size Right First

Anti chafing shorts need to fit snugly — loose shorts roll and bunch, which defeats the purpose entirely. Before buying, measure your waist and hips and cross-reference the Amazon size chart on the product listing. When in doubt between two sizes, most reviewers recommend sizing up for all-day comfort.

Match the Fabric to Your Use Case

If you're wearing these under a dress to a summer event, lightweight and seamless is the priority. If you're using them for yoga or light workouts, look for a slightly thicker fabric with stretch recovery. The Yeblues shorts hit a solid middle ground — thin enough to disappear under clothing, sturdy enough for movement.

Check the Inseam Length

Inseam length determines how much thigh coverage you get. Shorter inseams (2–3 inches) are more discreet under mini skirts; longer inseams (5–7 inches) give more coverage for active use. Check the product specs on Amazon before ordering — the Yeblues listing includes measurements so you know exactly what you're getting.
