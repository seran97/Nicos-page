How to choose a minimalist watch

Pick a versatile band. Mesh or leather bands tend to pair well with both casual and business outfits.
Check water resistance. Even minimalist watches vary - confirm the rating matches your daily needs, like hand-washing splashes versus swimming.
Consider dial size. A smaller, cleaner face reads as more minimalist, while larger faces lean sportier.
