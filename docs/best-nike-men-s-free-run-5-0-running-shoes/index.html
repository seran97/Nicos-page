Frequently Asked Questions

What does the flexible sole design feel like compared to regular running shoes?

The Free Run's flex grooves let the shoe bend more naturally with your foot's movement, giving a more barefoot-like feel than a stiffer traditional trainer.

Is this good for beginners transitioning to minimalist running?

It's a gentler introduction than a fully minimalist shoe since it still offers some cushioning, making it a reasonable starting point, though easing into mileage gradually is still recommended.

Can I use these for gym workouts too, not just running?

Yes, many buyers use them for general gym training and light cross-training thanks to the flexible, grounded feel.

How's the arch support for people who need extra stability?

Arch support is moderate given the minimalist design philosophy, so runners who need significant stability control may want a more structured shoe instead.

Do they run true to size like other Nike Free models?

Sizing is generally consistent with other Nike Free releases, with most buyers finding their regular size works well.
