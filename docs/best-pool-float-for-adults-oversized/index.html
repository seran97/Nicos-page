Frequently Asked Questions

Can one float really work as a hammock, saddle, lounge chair and drifter?

Yes — the air chambers are shaped so you can lie flat, straddle it like a saddle, recline against the backrest, or just drift depending on how you inflate the sections.

How much weight can this oversized float hold?

Most buyers report it comfortably supports a single adult up to around 250 lbs, with the 3, 5, and 8-pack options letting groups share multiple floats at once.

How long does it take to inflate?

With an electric pump it takes about 3-5 minutes per float; by mouth or hand pump expect closer to 10-15 minutes given the multiple chambers.

Is the material puncture resistant?

It uses thicker PVC than typical dollar-store floats, which holds up well to normal pool use, though rough concrete edges or pet claws can still puncture it.

Does it come with a repair patch kit?

Some multi-packs include a small patch kit; if yours doesn't, a standard PVC pool patch kit works fine as a backup.
