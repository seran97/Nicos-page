Buying Guide: What to Check Before You Buy

Do a patch test first

Apply to one small area (like an ankle) and wait 24 hours before going full-body, especially if you've had streaking issues with self-tanners before.

Exfoliate before your first application

Dry patches on knees, elbows, and ankles absorb tanner unevenly — a quick scrub beforehand helps it go on smoother.

Build gradually over several days

Apply every 1-2 days rather than layering heavily at once; gradual formulas are designed for buildable color, not instant depth.
