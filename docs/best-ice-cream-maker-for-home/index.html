Buying Guide

Freeze the bowl in advance. Plan at least 12-24 hours ahead so the bowl is fully frozen before churning.
Chill your base mixture first. A cold mixture churns faster and produces a smoother texture.
Add mix-ins near the end. Fold in chocolate chips or fruit during the last few minutes of churning for even distribution.
