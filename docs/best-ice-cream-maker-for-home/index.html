Ice Cream Maker for Home Buying Guide: 3 Steps

Before you spend a dollar, run through these three questions to make sure you're buying the right product.

Check if you need one bowl or two

If you plan to make multiple batches in a day — for a party or just because everyone wants a different flavor — a two-bowl set like this one saves you from a long wait between batches while the bowl refreezes.

Bowl-style machines like this require the freezer bowl to sit in the freezer for 12-24 hours before use. Build that into your planning, especially if you're making ice cream for a same-day event.

Budget ice cream makers vary a lot in long-term durability. Look for reviews mentioning use after several months, not just first-batch impressions, before deciding this fits your usage frequency.
