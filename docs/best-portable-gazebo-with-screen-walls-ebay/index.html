Portable Gazebo With Screen Walls Buying Guide: 3 Steps

What to check before ordering replacement mesh for your gazebo.

Measure Your Existing Gazebo Frame

Replacement netting needs to roughly match your current gazebo's dimensions to fit properly. Measure panel height and width before ordering to avoid a mismatch.

Check What's Included

Some replacement kits include sandbags or anchor points, others don't. If your gazebo has always felt unstable in wind, prioritize a kit that includes ground anchoring.

Weigh Repair Versus Replacement Cost

If only one or two panels are damaged, replacement netting is almost always cheaper than buying an entirely new gazebo. Compare the repair cost against a new unit before deciding.
