FAQ

What makes this considered a 'natural' dog food?

It's air-dried rather than heavily processed, uses whole-food ingredients like wild fish and turkey, and skips a lot of the artificial fillers found in mainstream kibble.

Does natural food cost more than regular kibble?

Generally yes, natural and minimally processed formulas run higher per pound than mass-market kibble, reflecting the ingredient quality.

Do I need to add water or anything before serving?

It can be served as-is, though some owners like to rehydrate a portion slightly for picky eaters or senior dogs.

Is it grain-inclusive or grain-free?

This particular formula is grain-free and built around superfood ingredients rather than traditional grains or fillers.

How should I store an opened bag?

Keep it sealed in a cool, dry place, ideally in an airtight container, since air-dried food can lose freshness faster than kibble once opened.
