Frequently Asked Questions

Does mineral sunscreen leave a white cast on darker skin tones?

Tinted mineral formulas like this one are designed to blend into medium-to-tan skin tones, reducing the classic chalky cast you get with plain zinc oxide sunscreens.

Is SPF 50 mineral sunscreen safe for daily facial use?

Yes, mineral (physical) sunscreens sit on top of the skin rather than absorbing into it, which dermatologists generally consider gentler for everyday use, including on sensitive or acne-prone skin.

Can I wear this mineral sunscreen under makeup?

Yes, the BB-cream-like finish is formulated to double as a light base, so most buyers apply it as the last step before or in place of foundation.

How often should I reapply SPF 50 sunscreen on my face?

Dermatology guidance generally recommends reapplying every two hours of direct sun exposure, or immediately after swimming or heavy sweating.

Will this sunscreen clog pores or cause breakouts?

Mineral formulas are typically non-comedogenic and lighter on the skin than heavy chemical sunscreens, though everyone's skin reacts differently, so a patch test is always a smart first step.
