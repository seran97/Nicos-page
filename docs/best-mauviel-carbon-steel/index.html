Buying Guide: How to Choose

  • Decide between clad stainless and true carbon steel

    Clad stainless like this pan needs no seasoning; raw carbon steel builds a non-stick patina over time but requires ongoing care.

  • Check the oven-safe rating

    If you plan to finish dishes under the broiler or in a hot oven, confirm the pan's max temperature first.

  • Preheat before adding oil

    This single habit prevents the vast majority of sticking complaints on any stainless surface, this pan included.
