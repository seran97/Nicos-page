Tri Tip Steak Buying Guide: 3 Steps

A quick framework before you commit to a purchase.

Plan thaw time before your cookout

Frozen aged steaks need proper time in the fridge to thaw safely, so order and plan at least a day or two ahead of your event.

Don't skip the resting step after grilling

Aged steaks benefit from resting a few minutes off the heat before cutting, which helps retain juiciness that a rushed serving can lose.

A handful of complaints center on shipping condition rather than the meat itself, so reviews mentioning delivery experience are worth a look.
