Will this hold up during a full week of daily summer walks?

Most owners report the fabric keeps its cooling ability after repeated daily soak-and-wear cycles across a whole season, as long as it gets a light rinse now and then.

What's the difference between this and a regular sun shirt?

A sun shirt only blocks UV rays; this vest adds evaporative cooling on top of SPF protection, so it actively lowers body temperature rather than just shielding skin.

Can I leave it on during car rides?

Yes, many owners keep it on for car rides in hot weather since it helps dogs stay comfortable even with the AC working overtime.

Does it work for short-haired breeds too?

Yes, short-haired dogs actually feel the cooling effect faster since there's less coat between the vest and their skin.

How do I stop my dog from trying to shake it off?

A snug, properly measured fit prevents most shaking attempts; loose vests are the main reason dogs try to wriggle out of them.
