FAQs

Why buy just filters instead of a full parts kit?

Filters wear out faster than brushes for most households, so buying a dedicated 6-pack lets you replace them more often without repurchasing a whole kit each time.

How long does one HEPA filter typically last?

Most owners replace filters every 4-6 weeks with daily vacuum use, sooner if you have pets shedding heavily.

Can I rinse and reuse these filters?

HEPA filters generally aren't meant to be washed since it can damage the filtration material, so treat these as disposable and replace rather than rinse.

Will a clogged filter actually hurt suction that much?

Yes, a clogged filter is one of the most common causes of gradually declining suction, and swapping it in fresh often restores performance immediately.
