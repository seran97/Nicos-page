Buying Guide: What To Look For

  1. Check the wax type

    Soy or coconut wax blends burn cleaner and more evenly than paraffin, and tend to hold scent longer without tunneling.

  2. Compare advertised burn time per candle, not per set

    A 200-hour claim across a 4-pack means roughly 50 hours each — do the math so you know what you're actually getting.

  3. Read reviews for scent longevity, not just initial throw

    Plenty of candles smell great out of the box but fade fast. Look for reviews specifically mentioning week two and beyond.
