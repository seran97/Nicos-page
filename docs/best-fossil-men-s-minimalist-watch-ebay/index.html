Will this strap fit every Fossil Minimalist watch?

It's built for 20mm lug widths, which covers most Fossil Minimalist models, but you should double-check your watch's specific lug width before ordering.

Is silicone comfortable for all-day wear?

Yes, silicone rally straps are popular specifically because they're soft, flexible, and breathable compared to stiffer leather bands.

Yes, silicone is naturally water-resistant and doesn't degrade the way leather does with sweat or occasional water exposure.

It leans casual-sporty, so it pairs best with everyday or weekend looks rather than a strict formal office outfit.
