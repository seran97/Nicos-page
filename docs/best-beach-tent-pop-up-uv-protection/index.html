Buying Guide: 3 Steps to the Right Beach Tent

  1. Check the UPF Rating First

    Not all "UV protection" claims are equal. Look specifically for UPF 50+ — this is the certified standard that blocks over 98% of UVA and UVB radiation. Anything below UPF 30 offers minimal real-world protection on a bright beach day. The Gorich meets the UPF 50+ standard, which is why it's our top pick in this category.

  2. Match the Size to Your Group

    Beach tents are often marketed optimistically. A "4-person" tent comfortably fits 2–3 adults with gear. If you're going with a family of four, size up to the 6-7 person option. The Gorich comes in multiple configurations so you can pick the right fit without overpaying for space you don't need.

  3. Verify the Anchoring System

    A beach tent without proper anchoring is a liability on a windy day. Look for at least 4 sand anchors plus sandbag pockets. The Gorich includes 5 sand anchors, which is above average for this price range and gives you real confidence in breezy conditions. Always stake it down even on calm days — beach weather changes fast.
