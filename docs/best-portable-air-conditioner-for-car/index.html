Buying Guide

Check the power source. Confirm it fits your car's 12V outlet or USB port without needing an adapter.
Consider humidity levels. Evaporative coolers work best in dry climates; humid regions will see less dramatic cooling.
Look at tank size and refill frequency. A larger tank means fewer stops to top off water on long drives.
