Buying Guide

How to Choose the Right BBQ Grill Cover

Not all grill covers are created equal. Follow these three steps before you buy.

  1. Confirm Your Exact Grill Model

    The single biggest mistake buyers make is purchasing a "universal" cover that doesn't actually fit. Before anything else, check the model number on your grill (usually on a sticker near the hopper or on the bottom). For Pit Boss owners, this cover fits the Austin XL, 1000S, 1000SC, 1000SQ, 1000SC2, 1100 Pro, and the 1000/1100/1150/1500 series. If your model is on that list, you're good to go.

  2. Check the Material & Waterproofing Claims

    Look for covers that specify waterproof (not just "water-resistant") material. Heavy-duty 600D polyester or Oxford fabric with a PVC coating is the standard for reliable protection. UV resistance is a bonus — it prevents the cover from cracking or fading after a season outdoors. This pick checks all those boxes at a price that won't make you wince.

  3. Look for Secure Fastening in Wind

    A cover that blows off in the first storm is useless. Adjustable velcro straps, buckles, or drawstrings at the bottom are non-negotiable if you live anywhere with regular wind. Check reviewer photos on Amazon — real buyers often post pictures showing how the straps look in use, which is more useful than any product description.
