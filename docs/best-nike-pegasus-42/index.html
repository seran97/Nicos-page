Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Pegasus 42 suitable for both easy runs and tempo workouts?

Yes, the Pegasus line has long been known as a versatile daily trainer that handles both easy recovery runs and faster tempo efforts reasonably well.

How does the Pegasus 42 compare to previous Pegasus versions?

Reviewers note incremental improvements in cushioning responsiveness over earlier versions, while keeping the familiar reliable ride the line is known for.

Is this a good first pair of proper running shoes?

It's often recommended as a solid starting point for newer runners thanks to its balanced cushioning and neutral support profile.

How many miles should I expect before replacing this shoe?

Most runners get around 300 to 500 miles out of the Pegasus before the cushioning noticeably breaks down.

Does it work well for wider feet?

The standard width fits most feet comfortably, though those with notably wide feet may want to check if a wide version is available for their size.
