Buying Guide: What To Look For

  1. Think in terms of total coverage, not just one candle

    If you have an open floor plan or multiple rooms, a multi-pack of smaller candles often beats one large candle for actual scent coverage.

  2. Compare cost per burn hour across the pack

    Divide the total price by total advertised hours to see which set is actually the better long-term value.

  3. Check wax composition for soot and cleanliness

    Soy or soy-paraffin blends tend to burn cleaner than pure paraffin, which matters if you're burning multiple candles regularly.
