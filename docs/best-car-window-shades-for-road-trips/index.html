Buying Guide: What to Look For

  1. Measure your window first

    Side windows vary a surprising amount between sedans, SUVs, and minivans, so check the height and width before ordering.

  2. Check the mounting method

    Suction cups work great on smooth glass but can struggle on tinted or textured windows — static cling is a solid backup.

  3. Look for UV + heat blocking, not just glare

    Cheap shades cut glare but let heat through; look for ones that specifically mention UV and infrared blocking.
