Buying Guide

Measure your foot in the afternoon. Feet swell during the day, so sizing then gives a more accurate fit for running shoes.
Check the width option. Standard vs. wide can make a big difference in comfort over long distances.
Break them in gradually. Wear them on short runs first before committing to your longest weekly mileage.

FAQs

Is the Hoka Clifton 10 good for daily training?

Yes, the Clifton line is built as a lightweight daily trainer with balanced cushioning suited to easy and moderate mileage runs.

Does the Clifton 10 run true to size?

Most reviewers say it runs true to size, though a small number with wider feet size up half a size for extra room in the toe box.

How much does the Clifton 10 weigh?

The Clifton line is known for being one of the lighter max-cushion trainers on the market, typically under 9 ounces for a men's size 9.

Is this shoe good for wide feet?

The standard width fits medium feet well; if you have wide feet, check for a wide-width version before ordering.

Can I use this shoe for racing?

It's primarily designed for daily training rather than racing, though some runners use it for longer, easier-paced races like marathons.
