Frequently Asked Questions

Does this work on induction stoves?

Yes, carbon steel is magnetic and works well on induction, gas, electric, and even open flame.

Do I need to season it before first use?

Yes, a quick seasoning with oil and heat before first use builds the initial nonstick patina, and it improves with every cook after that.

Is carbon steel harder to maintain than nonstick?

It needs a light oil wipe after washing to prevent rust, but otherwise it's low maintenance and gets more nonstick over time, unlike coated pans that degrade.

Can I put it in the dishwasher?

No, dishwashing strips the seasoning and can cause rust; hand wash and dry immediately, then a light oil coat.

How hot can it get for searing?

Carbon steel handles very high heat well, making it ideal for searing steaks or stir-frying at temperatures that would warp thinner nonstick pans.
