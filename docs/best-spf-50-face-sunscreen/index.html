How To Choose: 3 Steps

Check the active ingredient

Zinc oxide and titanium dioxide mineral filters tend to be gentler for sensitive or acne-prone skin than chemical filters.

Confirm broad spectrum coverage

SPF only measures UVB protection — make sure the label also says 'broad spectrum' for UVA coverage that prevents premature aging.

Test on your jawline first

Apply a small amount along your jawline for a day or two to check for white cast, breakouts, or irritation before committing to the full bottle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does mineral SPF 50 sunscreen leave a white cast?

Some white cast is common with zinc oxide formulas, especially right after application, but most fades as it fully absorbs into skin over a few minutes.

How often should I reapply face sunscreen?

Dermatologists generally recommend reapplying every 2 hours when outdoors, and after swimming or heavy sweating, regardless of the SPF number.
