How to choose a face sunscreen in 4 questions

1. Mineral or chemical filters?

Mineral (zinc oxide, titanium dioxide) sits on the skin and is usually gentler for sensitive or reactive skin, but can leave a white cast. Chemical filters absorb UV and tend to look invisible on all skin tones. Both protect when applied correctly.

2. What is your skin type?

Oily or acne-prone skin does best with oil-free, lightweight fluids or gels. Dry skin likes formulas with glycerin, ceramides or hyaluronic acid. Rosacea and very sensitive skin usually tolerate zinc-based mineral formulas best.

3. Do you want a tint?

Tinted mineral sunscreens hide the white cast and add light coverage. They also contain iron oxides, which help against visible light, useful if you deal with dark spots or melasma.

4. Will you reapply?

Sunscreen wears off. Reapply every 2 hours outdoors and after swimming or sweating. If you spend time in water, choose a water-resistant formula (40 or 80 minutes).

What SPF 50 really means

SPF measures protection against UVB, the rays that cause sunburn. In lab conditions SPF 30 blocks about 97% of UVB and SPF 50 about 98%. The bigger difference in real life is the amount you apply: most people use a quarter to a half of the tested amount. For the face, a common guide is about a quarter teaspoon, or two finger-lengths of product. Always look for broad spectrum on the label, which means it also protects against UVA, the rays linked to skin aging.

Frequently asked questions

Is SPF 50 better than SPF 30 for the face?

Slightly. SPF 30 blocks about 97% of UVB and SPF 50 about 98%. SPF 50 helps more if you burn easily, have dark spots or spend long hours outdoors, but applying enough and reapplying matters more than the number.

Which sunscreen does not leave a white cast?

Chemical sunscreens such as Supergoop! Unseen leave no cast. Among mineral options, tinted formulas like La Roche-Posay Anthelios Mineral Light Fluid and lightweight fluids like ISDIN Eryfotona leave the least.

What is the best sunscreen for acne-prone skin?

Look for oil-free, non-comedogenic formulas. EltaMD UV Clear, with zinc oxide and niacinamide, is the most common choice among people with breakouts and redness.

Can I use body sunscreen on my face?

You can, but body formulas are often thicker and more likely to sting the eyes or clog pores. If you use one, pick an oil-free, lightweight option such as a dry-touch lotion.

Do I need sunscreen indoors or on cloudy days?

Most UVA passes through clouds and window glass. If you sit near windows or go outside during the day, daily sunscreen is worth it.

How long does sunscreen last after opening?

Most sunscreens are made to stay effective for about three years if there is no expiration date. If it has an expiration date, follow it, and discard any sunscreen that changed color, smell or texture.
