Buying Guide: How to Choose

  • Measure your seats and count rows

    Grab a tape measure and note whether you need front-only, full 5-seat, or a bench-seat configuration before ordering.

  • Decide waterproof vs. breathable

    Leather-style covers wipe clean fast but run warmer; mesh and cotton blends breathe better in hot climates.

  • Check the installation style

    Clip-and-strap systems hold up far better over time than covers that rely on stretch alone.
