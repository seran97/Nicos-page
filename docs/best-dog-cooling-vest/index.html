FAQs

Does a dog cooling vest actually lower body temperature?

Evaporative cooling vests work by wicking moisture and using airflow to pull heat away from the dog's core as the water evaporates, which can measurably lower skin surface temperature during hot walks or outdoor activity.

How long does the cooling effect last once soaked?

Most owners report 2 to 3 hours of noticeable cooling in dry heat, less in humid conditions since evaporation slows down. Re-wetting the vest refreshes the effect almost instantly.

Will the vest make my dog wet and uncomfortable indoors?

It's designed for damp-not-dripping use, but it's still a wet garment, so it's best for outdoor walks, yard time, or car rides rather than lounging on furniture.

How do I pick the right size for my dog?

Measure girth (widest part of the ribcage) rather than weight alone, since breeds carry weight differently. When between sizes, most reviewers say size up for comfort.

Is it safe for dogs with skin sensitivities?

The fabric is generally breathable and soft, but any new vest should be introduced gradually and checked for chafing or irritation, especially around the legs and neck.
