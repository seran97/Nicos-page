Buying guide: 3 things to check first

  • Do a full paint decontamination first — Clay bar and paint correction beforehand make a bigger difference than the coating product itself.
  • Apply thin, even layers — Overapplying ceramic coating causes high spots and streaking that are hard to correct after curing.
  • Plan for a maintenance routine — Regular pH-neutral washes and occasional topper products extend coating life significantly.
