Buying Guide: What To Look For

  1. Confirm it's actually waterproof, not just splash-resistant

    Check the listing for a specific submersion or shower-use claim — a lot of cheaper brushes only tolerate light splashes and will short out in the shower.

  2. Check replacement head availability and cost

    The brush itself is a one-time buy, but heads wear out. Make sure replacements are easy to find before you commit to a brand.

  3. Match bristle firmness to your skin type

    Softer bristles for sensitive or acne-prone skin, firmer for normal skin that can handle more exfoliation.
