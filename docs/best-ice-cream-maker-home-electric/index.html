Buying Guide

Stock up on ice and rock salt. Plan to have enough on hand for the full churn cycle, especially for large batches.
Pack the ice-salt layers correctly. Alternate layers of ice and rock salt around the canister for even freezing.
Let it harden after churning. Transfer to the freezer for an hour or two after churning for a firmer scoopable texture.
