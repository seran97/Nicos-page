Buying Guide: How to Choose

  • Measure your actual height plus a few inches

    A mat should extend beyond your fully stretched-out body, not match it exactly.

  • Pick a thickness based on your joints

    1/2" suits sensitive knees and hips, while 1/4" gives more stability for balance poses.

  • Check your storage space before buying

    Oversized mats need a proper strap or a wider closet corner, not just any yoga bag.
