Buying Guide

  1. Match the liner to your lip tone, not your lipstick
    A liner close to your natural lip color blends more seamlessly under any lipstick shade.
  2. Prep lips before applying
    A light lip balm underneath helps the formula glide without tugging.
  3. Line first, then fill for longer wear
    Filling your whole lip with liner before adding lipstick helps the color last through the day.
