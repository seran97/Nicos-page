Buying Guide: 3 Steps to Choosing the Right Mosquito Net

  1. Measure Your Bed & Ceiling Height

    Before purchasing any mosquito net for a queen size bed, measure your bed dimensions (typically 60" × 80") and your ceiling height. Rectangular canopy nets like this one require adequate overhead clearance — ideally at least 7 feet — to hang properly and provide full 360° coverage without bunching at the sides. Confirm the net's drop length matches your needs.

  2. Evaluate Mesh Density & Airflow

    Mesh density determines both protection level and breathability. A finer mesh blocks smaller insects and provides better protection, but can reduce airflow in hot climates. The Premium Bed Mosquito Net strikes an excellent balance — its breathable mesh keeps air circulating while maintaining a tight enough weave to block mosquitoes, gnats, and other common bedroom pests effectively.

  3. Consider Installation Method & Setup Ease

    Rectangular canopy nets typically hang from four ceiling points using hooks or a central suspension ring. Check whether your ceiling can accommodate hooks (most can with simple hardware). The Premium Bed Mosquito Net is designed for straightforward installation — most users report setup in under 15 minutes. If ceiling mounting isn't possible, look for freestanding frame alternatives, though these typically cost significantly more.
