FAQs

How do I confirm this fits my exact Dreame model?

Check the model number printed on the underside of your vacuum against the listing's compatibility list before ordering, since sub-models sometimes differ in part shape.

How often should filters and brushes be swapped?

Most owners replace filters every 2-3 months and brushes every 3-6 months depending on how much pet hair and daily runtime the vacuum handles.

Are these official Dreame parts?

No, these are third-party compatible parts, not OEM Dreame components, which is part of why the price is significantly lower.

Could using these void my vacuum's warranty?

It's possible in some cases, so if your vacuum is still under active warranty, check the manufacturer's consumable-parts policy before swapping anything out.

What are the signs a mop pad needs replacing?

Visible fraying, streaking on floors instead of clean wipes, or a pad that's gone noticeably thin are the clearest signals it's time for a fresh one.
