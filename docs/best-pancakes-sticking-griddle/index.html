Quick Buying Guide

Check the base material. Tri-ply stainless conducts heat more evenly than thin aluminum, which is the root cause of most sticking.
Preheat properly. A cold or unevenly heated griddle is the #1 reason pancakes tear when flipped.
Use a light fat layer. Even non-stick surfaces benefit from a thin coat of butter or oil for browning and easy release.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do my pancakes stick to the griddle even with butter?

Usually it's uneven heat or a worn-out non-stick coating. A properly seasoned tri-ply surface with consistent heat distribution, like the COOKER KING griddle, solves most sticking problems without drowning food in oil.

A light coat is still recommended for flavor and browning, but you won't need to soak the pan like you would with a cheap non-stick surface.

How do I clean it after making pancakes?

Warm water and a soft sponge is usually enough. It's dishwasher safe too, though hand washing preserves the finish longer.

Will this work for crepes as well as pancakes?

Yes, the flat wide surface and even heat retention make it well suited for both crepes and thicker pancakes.
