Frequently Asked Questions

Does the mirror still work as a normal rearview mirror?

Yes, it clips over your existing mirror and shows the live rear camera feed by default, but you can toggle back to a standard reflective mirror when needed.

What does ADAS actually warn me about?

Built-in ADAS flags lane departure and blind spot activity using the front camera's field of view, giving audible alerts before you drift or change lanes into traffic.

Will it fit over my factory mirror?

Adjustable rubber straps fit most standard rearview mirrors, though unusually large or curved factory mirrors may need a slightly different mounting angle.

Does GPS need a phone connection?

No, GPS is built directly into the unit and logs location and speed independently.

How's the rear camera at night?

The 2.5K rear camera holds up reasonably well under streetlights, though it's noticeably softer than the front 4K feed in near-total darkness.
