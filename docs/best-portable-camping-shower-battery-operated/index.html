Yes, the pump submerges in any bucket, jug, or collapsible water container and pushes water up through the attached hose and shower head.

It shows remaining battery level and water temperature at a glance, so you know how much shower time you have left before it's time to recharge.

Can I charge it while camping without an outlet?
