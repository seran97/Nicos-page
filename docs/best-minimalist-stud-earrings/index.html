How to choose minimalist stud earrings

Check the metal. Sterling silver or gold-plated stainless steel are safer bets for sensitive ears than base metals.
Look at stone setting. Prong-set or bezel-set stones tend to stay secure longer than glued accents.
Consider a set over a single pair. Buying a small rotation means you're never stuck without a backup if one earring gets lost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these minimalist stud earrings hypoallergenic?

Sterling silver is generally well tolerated by sensitive ears, though anyone with a nickel allergy should always double-check the specific listing's metal purity.

Will the cubic zirconia stones lose their sparkle over time?

With proper care - avoiding lotions and perfumes directly on the stones and storing them separately - CZ studs typically hold their shine for years.

Can I shower or swim while wearing sterling silver studs?

Brief exposure to water is fine, but prolonged contact with chlorine or salt water can dull the shine over time, so it's best to remove them before swimming.

Do these come as a single pair or a set?

This particular listing includes a small assortment of minimalist designs, which makes it a good option for gifting or building an everyday-earring rotation.

How do I keep sterling silver earrings from tarnishing?

Store them in an airtight pouch away from humidity, and give them a gentle polish with a silver cloth every few weeks to keep them looking new.
