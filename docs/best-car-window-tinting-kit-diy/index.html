Buying Guide: What to Look For

  1. Check what's actually included

    Most kits are tools only, not film — confirm you're buying film separately before you start.

  2. Prioritize a felt or soft-edge squeegee

    Hard plastic edges are the most common cause of scratched film during install.

  3. Practice on a flat piece of glass first

    Curved rear windows are unforgiving for beginners — get comfortable with technique on a flat window first.
