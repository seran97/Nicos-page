Why I even wrote this

Bath time with our shepherd mix used to be an actual ordeal because half the loose fur would just clump up wet and stick everywhere instead of rinsing off cleanly. I tried a regular bath brush but the bristles were too stiff and he'd squirm away every time. A dog walker I chat with recommended trying washing gloves instead of a brush, since they let you scrub gently while still getting a grip on a wet, wriggly dog. This 3-piece set turned out to be a decent budget option, and having a spare glove meant I didn't have to stop mid-bath when one got too soapy to hold onto properly. Bath time is still not his favorite thing, but at least the fur actually rinses down the drain now instead of clumping in the tub.
