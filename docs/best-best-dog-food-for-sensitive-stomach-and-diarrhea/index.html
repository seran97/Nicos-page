FAQ

Will this food actually help my dog's diarrhea?

Many owners with dogs prone to loose stools report firmer stools within a week or two of switching, though you should transition gradually and consult your vet for chronic issues.

Is air-dried food easier on sensitive stomachs than kibble?

Air-dried formulas retain more natural nutrients and tend to be gentler to digest than heavily processed kibble, which is why they're popular for sensitive-stomach dogs.

How do I transition my dog to this food?
