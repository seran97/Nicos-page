Outdoor String Lights Edison Bulb Buying Guide: 3 Steps

Replacing a single Edison bulb sounds simple until you're staring at five listings with slightly different specs — here's what actually matters.

Check that the listing specifies S14 shape and E27 base, or measure your existing bulb, before ordering to avoid a mismatch.

Confirm an Outdoor IP65 Rating

Indoor-only decorative bulbs aren't built for outdoor moisture exposure, so confirm the listing explicitly mentions outdoor or waterproof use.

Since shipping can take a while, ordering 2-3 spare bulbs upfront saves you from repeating this search next time one cracks.
