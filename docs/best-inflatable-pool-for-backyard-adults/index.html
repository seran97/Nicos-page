Frequently Asked Questions

How much yard space do I need for a backyard adult pool like this?
You'll want a flat area roughly 17 by 8 feet, plus a couple feet of clearance around the edges for sitting or stepping in. Grass, patio, or a foam ground mat all work fine underneath.
How long does it take to fill an oversized inflatable pool?
With a standard garden hose, expect 1.5 to 2.5 hours depending on your water pressure. Filling in the early morning avoids a long wait during peak sun hours.
Will this pool support adults without tipping or leaking?
Yes, the reinforced PVC walls and multi-chamber air rings are built for adult weight loads, a durability point people specifically ask about on r/deals.
How do I keep the water clean without a full filtration system?
A pool cover when not in use, a small floating chlorine dispenser, and draining/refreshing every 3-5 days keeps things clear for a backyard setup like this.
