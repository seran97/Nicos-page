Buying Guide

  1. Rinse the bladder before first use
    New hydration bladders can carry a plastic taste; a rinse with baking soda water resolves this quickly.
  2. Adjust the vest straps snug, not tight
    A vest-style pack should feel secure without restricting breathing during exertion.
  3. Plan refill points on longer routes
    2L covers most half-day outings, but plan refill stops for anything longer.
