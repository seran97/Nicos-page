Frequently Asked Questions

Are Nike trail running shoes good for rocky terrain?

Yes, most Nike trail models feature reinforced outsoles and lugged rubber for grip on rocky and uneven trails.

Can I use these shoes for both road and trail running?

They're optimized for trails, but light road use is fine occasionally - just expect faster tread wear on pavement.

How do Nike trail shoes fit compared to road shoes?

They tend to fit slightly snugger through the midfoot for better lockdown on technical terrain.

Are they waterproof?

Most trail models are water-resistant rather than fully waterproof, though some come in a Gore-Tex version.

How often should I replace trail running shoes?

Typically every 300-400 miles, sooner if you run on abrasive rocky trails often.
